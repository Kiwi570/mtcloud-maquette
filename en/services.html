Questions

Your questions, my answers.

Is there a subscription?

No. You pay once for the installation and the box, depending on the configuration you choose. No yearly price rises.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes: photos back up automatically from iOS and Android, with the mobile app.

What about when I'm away from home?

With the Everywhere access option (+€50), you can reach your photos and files anywhere in the world, through a secure personal address (HTTPS).

Who can see my photos?

You, and the people you share an album with. Your data stays on your home network: nobody views, analyses or resells it.

Does the box use much power?

About 6 W, running continuously. It is silent and sits next to your internet router.

I don't live on the Riviera.

The box can be shipped to you pre-configured anywhere in France, with remote support.

What if it needs updating or fixing?

I stay reachable after the installation: updates, troubleshooting and hardware upgrades.
