Why MTCloud

Why I created MTCloud.

In over 15 years of IT, I kept seeing the same stories:

  • photos lost because a subscription was not renewed
  • accounts closed without notice
  • prices going up every year
  • family memories analysed for advertising

Technology should serve people, not the other way round.

At home, yet everywhere

Keeping your data at home doesn't cut you off from the world.

  • On your phone

    Photos back up on their own.

  • On your TV

    No cables, no USB stick.

  • From a browser

    At home or on the go.
